This is your first post. To write a new one, add a file to the `_posts` folder named
`YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md`, copy the few lines at the top of this file, and write below them.

## Markdown works

You can use **bold**, *italics*, [links](https://example.com), lists:

- one idea
- another idea

> And quotes, for when someone said it better.

Images go in `assets/img/` and are added like this:

`![A description](/assets/img/your-image.jpg)`
