Hi, I’m Ana!

I’m a multidisciplinary researcher and designer working at the intersection of synthetic biology, bioengineering and human–computer interaction. I’m interested in how we can engineer biological systems to sense, respond and reveal new information, while making scientific tools more intuitive and accessible.

My current work spans molecular biosensing, computational protein design, immunoengineering and laboratory automation. Across these fields, I move between the molecular and the human scale: from engineering DNA- and protein-based systems to exploring how people interact with scientific technologies.

My approach is human-centred and inspired by nature. I’m fascinated by the complexity and elegance of living systems, and by the possibilities of learning from and engineering them. Nature is often where my ideas begin; people are why I pursue them.
