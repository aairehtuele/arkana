Hi, I’m Ana!

I’m a multidisciplinary researcher and designer working across bioengineering, human–computer interaction and computational materials science, currently focused on synthetic biology, immunoengineering and lab automation.

I combine biosensors, automation and design to make science easier to do and easier to access: building tools that support scientists at the bench and open research up to more people.

My work is human-centered and inspired by nature. People are why I do it, and nature, from biomolecules up, is where many of my ideas begin.
